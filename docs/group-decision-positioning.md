# Moodish: group decisions before checkout

12 October 2026. Differentiation is a product hypothesis, not a claim that no competitor offers these features.

Swiggy already supports shared-cart group ordering: https://blog.swiggy.com/life-at-swiggy/heres-how-swiggy-built-the-group-ordering-feature/
Zomato for Enterprise offers employee meals and company controls: https://enterprise.zomato.com/
HungerBox offers cafeteria operations: https://www.hungerbox.ai/products-new/

Moodish should focus on a private, channel-accessible decision workflow for mixed-preference groups: actual attendance, checked meal choices, explicit uncertainty, current all-in caps and accountable purchasing. Shared carts and multiple restaurant checkout are fulfilment features we can consume if authorized, rather than claim as our advantage.

This increment adds a decision brief to the authenticated meal view: actual joining/skipping/pending subscription counts, current budget limits, per-person cost, remaining budget, stale quote warnings, and the lowest-cost eligible checked option. It never auto-selects or orders and does not publish dietary answers or identify participants in the brief. Capacity is not an invitation count. Estimates do not guarantee allergy safety, availability, or delivery alignment.

Not yet implemented: equivalent Microsoft Teams workspace integration, automated multi-restaurant ordering, billing, or synchronized arrivals. Existing Slack/Discord/WhatsApp integrations still require activation. Live provider use remains blocked until the Swiggy agreement record and callback are cleared.
